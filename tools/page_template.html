SONIX

Audition clips for the Sonix library, grouped by the panel's categories. Each instrument plays through a stand-in for the Sonix engine (key and velocity zones, repitching, loops, envelope) with a light reverb, mostly on the same I–vi–IV–V progression in C at 92 BPM. Clips marked NEW are from the second batch.

What to listen for

  • Whether each sound feels expensive or cheap, and which ones you want more of.
  • Acoustic clips (pianos, harp, mallets) sit a few dB quieter than the synths because they keep their full dynamics.
  • The real plugin adds the filter, LFO, chorus and delay on top. These clips are dry samples plus reverb.
  • Every recorded set is mapped by measured pitch, not by its file names. Several libraries name notes an octave or two away from where they sound.
  • The kalimba and tubular bells are retuned per note to equal temperament so they play in key with everything else.
