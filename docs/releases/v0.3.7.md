Sonix is a ROMpler for MPC standalone devices: 128 presets, 45 instruments, four layers per preset, built-in effects and a touch faceplate in eleven colorways.

![MAIN page](https://github.com/3dthc/Sonix/raw/main/docs/images/main_browse.jpg)

## What's in it

- **128 presets in 12 banks**, also listed in MPC's own PRESET list. Every preset plays the moment you pick it.
- **Four layers per preset**: sound, level, pan, octave, fine, on and solo for each.
- **Sound shaping**: filter with envelope and key tracking, amp envelope, LFO (filter, vibrato, tremolo), chorus, tempo-synced delay, reverb.
- **Preset browser on the screen**: banks on the left, presets on the right. ◀ ▶, the data wheel and Q-Link 16 step through the bank.
- **Favourites**: ★ FAV stars a preset, and ★ FAVORITES lists them all (11 per page).
- **Eleven colorways**: Midnight, Platinum, Studio Grey, Purple, Yellow, Gold, Green, Camo, Metallic Red, Silver, Ice Blue.
- **16 Q-Links on every page**, in four columns of four.

## The pages

- **MAIN**: the knobs (filter, amp envelope, LFO, FX & output) around a screen with two views. BROWSE picks presets. LAYERS shows the waveform and the four layers; tap a layer to turn it on or off.
- **LAYERS**: one strip per layer to change its sound and set level, pan, octave and fine.
- **SETTINGS**: tuning, playing, delay and reverb knobs, plus colorway, waveform animation, mod wheel and velocity curve.

## Install

Build it with the one command in the README (WSL + Docker Desktop). It produces `Sonix-0.3.7-mpc-armv7.zip`. Install that on the MPC like any other plugin, then restart the MPC. SETTINGS should show VERSION 0.3.7.

Sounds: VSCO 2 CE, Versilian Community Sample Library, Meta Open Sound (CC0) and the Salamander Grand Piano (CC BY 3.0); see `library/LICENSES.md`.
