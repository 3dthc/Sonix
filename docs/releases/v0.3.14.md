## Changes

- **Projects are saved as one block.** A project now keeps the preset, your edits and every setting (tuning, theme,
  layers, voices, effects) together, and reopening it puts all of it back in one go. The edited marker shows
  straight away instead of a moment later.
- **Favourites are kept for good.** Every Sonix on the MPC shares one favourites list: a star stays in every project and
  every new instance until you take it off, shows in every open Sonix at once, and survives upgrades (it's kept in the
  plugin folder's `user/` folder). The first time you open projects saved with 0.3.12 or earlier, their favourites join the
  list; once you star or unstar something yourself, the list is yours and projects no longer change it.

## Fixes

- **Quicker to open, smoother right after.** The skin file MPC reads every time it opens Sonix is a third of its old
  size (3.9 MB to 1.2 MB, same layout). While Sonix reads the rest of its sound library in the background after it
  opens, that work now runs at a low priority, so the screen and page switches don't wait on it; the sounds a preset
  needs still load at full speed.
- A new Sonix (or a new project) no longer starts with an empty ★ FAVORITES list.
- Reopening a project no longer lands on a different preset. If the data wheel was the last thing used on the LCD
  (after touching a preset in the list, ◀ ▶ or a tab) before saving, the project used to open one preset further
  on, sometimes without your edits. Projects saved with earlier versions open on the right preset too.
- Projects saved with earlier versions still open with their sounds and settings.
