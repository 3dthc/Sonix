# Sonix v0.4.0

## What's new
- **Arpeggiator, patterns and chords:** a new ARP page (its own tab) with MODE (off / arp / pattern / chord), direction, rate (with dotted and triplet steps), latch, octaves, gate, swing and strum.
- **16 patterns:** Pulse, Rolling Octave, Offbeat Stab, Piano Comp, Trance Gate, Pluck Run, Minor Climb, Major Climb, Bass Drive, Strum Down, Ostinato 3-3-2, Heartbeat, Fifths Bounce, Shimmer, Half-Time Chords and Waltz.
- **8 chord types:** Major, Minor, Dom 7, Min 7, Maj 7, Sus 4, Power and Min 9, with an adjustable strum.
- **In time with the song:** the steps follow the host's song position and tempo, so patterns stay locked when you start, stop or jump around.
- **Sustain pedal and latch:** the pedal holds the arp's notes; LATCH keeps it playing after you let go.
- **Sound shaping on the same page:** cutoff, resonance, chorus, delay, reverb and release.
- Arp settings are saved with the project. Existing projects open unchanged.
